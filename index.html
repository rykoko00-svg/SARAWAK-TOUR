Welcome to Our Sarawak Tour !

Package Tour

3D2N Kuching Getaway

Quick city tour of Kuching's Historical White Rajah architecture, waterfront strolls and a day trip to the Semenggoh Wildlife Center to see orangutans.

4D3N Kuching & Damai Holiday Package

Combines Kuching city sights with a relaxing beach and rainforest escape at Damai, plus a visit to the Sarawak Culture Village.

5D4N Best of Kuching & Bako

In depth wildlife tracking and coastal hiking a Bako National Park, proboscis monkey spotting and orangutans encounters at Semenggoh.

6D5N Hearts of Sarawak

Kuching arrival evening Santubong Wildlife Cruise, Heart-to-Heart Orangutan program and multi-day cultural experience combining longhouse visits.

6D5N Kuching City Discovery with Fairy & Wind Cave

City strolls, Sarawak River cruise, exploration of the mystical limestone cavern at Bau Fairy & Cave and a mangrove cruise.

7D6N Sarawak Explorer Package

Spans Kuching, Miri and Mulu National Park featuring ancient show caves (Deer, Lang, Wind and Clearwater Caves), bat exodus viewings and traditional Iban longhouse stays.
